The Red Team Mission

The Red Team simulates real-world attacks to test the security posture of an organization. By thinking like an adversary, we identify critical vulnerabilities before malicious actors do.

  • Targeted Penetration Testing
  • Advanced Social Engineering
  • Exploit Research & Development

Operational Methodology

1. Recon: nmap -sC -sV target.local
2. Scan: Identifying service vulnerabilities...
3. Exploit: use exploit/windows/smb/ms17_010_eternalblue
4. Post: Initial access gained. Escalating privileges.
5. Cleanup: Wiping session logs.
